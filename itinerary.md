# China Trip — Itinerary

## Travelers
- Lilly — Chinese-Canadian, born/raised in Beijing until age 11, later became Canadian citizen, now lives in Zurich.
- Emmanuel — French citizen, boyfriend. Max 30 days in China per current visa-free entry policy for France.

## Key Dates & Constraints
- **Arrival:** Sept 30, 10:30am — Beijing Daxing Airport (connecting flight; risk of missed connection).
- **Lilly's departure from China:** Oct 28, 20:30, **Shanghai → Zurich**. (Booked from Shanghai, not Beijing — the trip now ends in Shanghai, no loop back to Beijing at the end.)
- **Emmanuel:** EU/French citizen — 30 days visa-free in China, no visa process needed. Window: Sept 30 → ~Oct 30.
- **After the joint trip:** Emmanuel continues on to Japan for ~1 month.
- **Lilly:** returns to Zurich, starts work **Nov 4**.
- Total time in China: 28 nights (Sept 30–Oct 28). Beijing leg uses 8 of those nights (fixed, Sept 30–Oct 8, matching Emmanuel's own draft calendar); 20 nights left for the southbound rail loop.
- **Oct 1–7 = Golden Week** (China's peak domestic travel holiday) — overlaps almost the entire Beijing stay (Oct 8 itself is just the travel day out). Expect major crowds/inflated prices; book timed-entry tickets (Forbidden City, Great Wall) well in advance.

## Route Preference
Rail-only (HSR G/D/C class) strongly preferred — no departures before 8am, no arrivals after 22:30, except explicitly-flagged car/bus transfers where no rail exists. Balance of culture, nature, cities, cuisine, and tech (Emmanuel: outdoorsy + culture/history + interested in China's tech scene).

## The Itinerary (12306-verified where noted; some legs flagged as approximate/bus)

Beijing → Xi'an (1-night stopover) → Chongqing (+ **Wulong Karst** day trip) → Kunming → **Shilin** (day trip) → Jianshui → **Yuanyang** (bus, flagged) → Kunming (same-day transit, no overnight) → **Dali** → Kunming (transit) → Guilin → **Longji** (day trip, bus, flagged) → Yangshuo → Guilin (transit) → Shanghai → **Suzhou** (1 night) → Shanghai (final — depart Oct 28, 20:30 to Zurich).

Rebuilt from the two-itinerary draft per Lilly's direction: single itinerary only (no numbering, no tabs). Dali is kept despite being a dead-end spur north of Kunming — the least-bad sequencing loops it into the Kunming-area cluster (Kunming → Shilin → Jianshui → Yuanyang → transit through Kunming → Dali → transit through Kunming → onward to Guilin), so Kunming gets passed through three times but every other leg stays a forward-moving HSR trunk line. Shilin, Longji, and now Wulong Karst are added as day trips (not relocations) so they don't cost extra nights beyond what's needed to fit them.

This revision (on top of the single-route rebuild) applies four changes confirmed by Lilly via live 12306 research: (1) Beijing departure shifts one day later, to Oct 8, to match Emmanuel's own draft calendar; (2) Xi'an is cut to a genuine 1-night stopover — Terracotta Army is deprioritized given Golden Week crowds, in favor of a quick City Wall/old town/food evening; (3) Chongqing is extended by a night to fit a full-day Wulong Karst trip (Three Natural Bridges, Furong Cave — part of the UNESCO South China Karst site), chosen over Zhangjiajie for being less touristy and scenically distinct, and made easy by the new Chongqing East → Wulong South HSR link (~38 min); (4) the Kunming overnight between Yuanyang and Dali is dropped — Jianshui → Kunming → Dali connections run same-day and frequently, so Yuanyang → Dali is now one long transit day with Kunming as a pass-through, not a stay. Net effect: +1 night Beijing, −1 night Xi'an, +1 night Chongqing, −1 night Kunming — all four changes cancel out exactly, so every date from Dali onward is unchanged from the prior build.

**Two legs are genuinely not rail** and are flagged accordingly: Jianshui↔Yuanyang (no train serves Yuanyang at all — bus/car only, ~3h each way) and Guilin↔Longji (shuttle bus, ~2h45m–3h each way, no rail line). Everything else is HSR/intercity rail (G/D/C class), including the new Chongqing↔Wulong hop.

**Total rail time: ~35h across 13 rail/bus legs** (11 rail legs ≈ 31h10m HSR + 2 flagged bus legs ≈ 6h, plus the Wulong day-trip shuttle). Stay lengths: Xi'an 1 night, Chongqing 4, Kunming 2 (+ transit passes), Jianshui 1, Yuanyang 1, Dali 2, Guilin 2, Yangshuo 3, Shanghai 3, Suzhou 1 — 20 nights on the loop + 8 in Beijing = 28 nights total, Sept 30 → Oct 28, matching the fixed trip length exactly. The four Oct-8 changes (Beijing +1, Xi'an −1, Chongqing +1, Kunming-overnight −1) net to zero, so everything from Dali onward lands on the same dates as the prior build.

**On the "no pre-8am, no post-22:30" rule:** every rail leg above holds to it, **with one exception**: the Yuanyang→Dali same-day run on Oct 17 likely needs an early-morning bus out of Yuanyang (~05:30–06:00) to make the 09:02 Jianshui→Kunming train — a direct trade-off for dropping the Kunming overnight, flagged honestly rather than smoothed over. Two other combined travel days run long by necessity (Dali→Kunming→Guilin on Oct 19, and Yangshuo→Guilin→Shanghai on Oct 24) but still depart at/after 8am and land before 22:30 — full travel days with little else planned, flagged in the table above.
