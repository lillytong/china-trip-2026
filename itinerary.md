# China Trip — Itinerary

## Travelers
- Lilly — Chinese-Canadian, born/raised in Beijing until age 11, later became Canadian citizen, now lives in Zurich.
- Emmanuel — French citizen, boyfriend. Max 30 days in China per current visa-free entry policy for France.

## Key Dates & Constraints
- **Arrival:** Sept 30, 10:30am — Beijing Daxing Airport (connecting flight; risk of missed connection).
- **Lilly's departure from China:** Oct 28, 20:30, **Shanghai → Zurich**. (Booked from Shanghai, not Beijing — the trip now ends in Shanghai, no loop back to Beijing at the end.)
- **Emmanuel:** EU/French citizen — 30 days visa-free in China, no visa process needed. Window: Sept 30 → ~Oct 30.
- **After the joint trip:** Emmanuel continues on to Japan for ~1 month.
- **Lilly:** returns to Zurich, starts work **Nov 4**.
- Total time in China: 28 nights (Sept 30–Oct 28). Beijing leg uses 8 of those nights (fixed, Sept 30–Oct 8, matching Emmanuel's own draft calendar); 20 nights left for the southbound rail loop.
- **Oct 1–7 = Golden Week** (China's peak domestic travel holiday) — overlaps almost the entire Beijing stay (Oct 8 itself is just the travel day out). Expect major crowds/inflated prices; book timed-entry tickets (Forbidden City, Great Wall) well in advance.

## Route Preference
Rail-only (HSR G/D/C class) strongly preferred — **no departures before 9am** (tightened from the earlier 8am floor), no arrivals after 22:30, except explicitly-flagged car/bus transfers where no rail exists. Balance of culture, nature, cities, cuisine, and tech (Emmanuel: outdoorsy + culture/history + interested in China's tech scene).

## The Itinerary (12306-verified where noted; some legs flagged as approximate/bus)

Beijing → Xi'an (1-night stopover) → Chongqing (+ **Wulong Karst** day trip) → Kunming (+ **Shilin** and **Dali** day trips) → Jianshui → **Yuanyang** (bus, flagged) → Kunming (1-night return stopover, flagged) → Guilin → **Longji** (day trip, bus, flagged) → Yangshuo → Guilin (1-night return stopover) → Shanghai → **Suzhou** (1 night) → Shanghai (final — depart Oct 28, 20:30 to Zurich).

Rebuilt from the two-itinerary draft per Lilly's direction: single itinerary only (no numbering, no tabs). Dali is now a same-day round trip from Kunming rather than its own multi-night stop — Kunming↔Dali is an easy ~2h each way by frequent D-class HSR, so it slots in alongside the existing Shilin day trip without adding a dead-end spur to the route. The Kunming-area cluster reads as: Kunming (Shilin day trip, Dali day trip) → Jianshui → Yuanyang → back through Kunming (1-night stopover) → on to Guilin. Since Yuanyang has no rail at all, the route always returns to Kunming — the nearest HSR hub — before continuing by proper HSR, rather than chaining bus and rail straight through to Guilin in one very long day. Shilin, Dali, Longji, and Wulong Karst are all added as day trips (not relocations), so none of them costs extra nights beyond what's needed to fit them.

This revision (on top of the single-route rebuild) applies four changes confirmed by Lilly via live 12306 research: (1) Beijing departure shifts one day later, to Oct 8, to match Emmanuel's own draft calendar; (2) Xi'an is cut to a genuine 1-night stopover — Terracotta Army is deprioritized given Golden Week crowds, in favor of a quick City Wall/old town/food evening; (3) Chongqing is extended by a night to fit a full-day Wulong Karst trip (Three Natural Bridges, Furong Cave — part of the UNESCO South China Karst site), chosen over Zhangjiajie for being less touristy and scenically distinct, and made easy by the new Chongqing East → Wulong South HSR link (~38 min); (4) the Kunming overnight between Yuanyang and Dali is dropped — see the Dali rework below, which supersedes this point. Net effect of these three still-standing changes: +1 night Beijing, −1 night Xi'an, +1 night Chongqing — these cancel out exactly, so nothing shifts through the end of the Chongqing stay.

**Dali → Kunming day trip (earlier revision):** Dali is reworked from a 2-night dead-end stop (reached via a Kunming pass-through) into a single-day round trip from Kunming itself, folded into the same Kunming stay as the Shilin day trip. Kunming grows from 2 to 3 nights to host both day trips; the old Yuanyang→Dali leg and the old Dali→Kunming→Guilin leg collapse into a single leg, Yuanyang→Guilin via Kunming (same-day transit, no overnight, continuing straight on to Guilin instead of stopping at Dali). Net effect versus the prior build: Kunming +1 night, Dali −2 nights (dropped as a stand-alone stop) — a net one day freed up, added to Shanghai (3→4 nights) at the time. **This freed day has since been reassigned — see the next revision.**

**Latest revision (Kunming overnight restored on the return leg):** The same-day Yuanyang→Guilin sprint (bus to Jianshui, HSR to Kunming, big layover, HSR on to Guilin, ~16h door-to-door with a ~05:30 bus departure) was the one leg that broke the "no pre-8am" rule and was never realistic to book as drawn. Fixed per Lilly's direction: **the route always comes back to Kunming — the nearest HSR hub — before continuing on**, since Yuanyang has no rail at all. Yuanyang→Kunming (bus + C-class HSR) is now its own travel day, followed by a Kunming overnight, then Kunming→Guilin (G910, 17:10–21:38) the next afternoon — both legs comfortably inside the 8am–22:30 window. This costs one more night than the same-day version, so the day previously freed up by the Dali rework and given to Shanghai is redirected here instead: **Shanghai reverts to 3 nights (4→3)**, keeping the total at 28 nights, Sept 30 → Oct 28, with the fixed Oct 28, 20:30 Shanghai departure unchanged. Every date from Longji onward shifts one day later versus the prior build.

**Latest revision (Guilin restored as the return hub before Shanghai; 9am floor):** Same fix, second application. The Yangshuo→Shanghai leg was modeled as a same-day Yangshuo→Guilin→Shanghai pass-through (D8262 08:57 + G264 12:05) — but Guilin is always the start/end point for this cluster (Longji and Yangshuo both radiate from and return to it), so per Lilly's direction it now gets the same treatment as Kunming: an overnight stop, not a rushed connection. Yangshuo→Guilin and Guilin→Shanghai are now separate travel days. This costs one more night, funded by trimming Yangshuo from 3 to 2 nights (rather than compressing Shanghai a second time, having already gone 4→3 in the prior revision) — total stays at 28 nights, dates from Oct 23 onward unaffected beyond the split itself. Separately, the **"no pre-8am" rule tightens to "no pre-9am"** per Lilly's direction, which retroactively flags two existing legs as non-compliant pending 12306 re-verification: Yangshuo→Guilin (D8262, 08:57) and Kunming→Jianshui (C552, 08:23, previously justified as the only viable train before an 18:36 gap — may still be the one unavoidable exception).

**Two legs are genuinely not rail** and are flagged accordingly: Jianshui↔Yuanyang (no train serves Yuanyang at all — bus/car only, ~3h each way) and Guilin↔Longji (shuttle bus, ~2h45m–3h each way, no rail line). Everything else is HSR/intercity rail (G/D/C class), including the new Chongqing↔Wulong hop.
