# China Trip — Itinerary

## Travelers
- Lilly — Chinese-Canadian, born/raised in Beijing until age 11, later became Canadian citizen, now lives in Zurich.
- Emmanuel — French citizen, boyfriend. Max 30 days in China per current visa-free entry policy for France.

## Key Dates & Constraints
- **Arrival:** Sept 30, 10:30am — Beijing Daxing Airport (connecting flight; risk of missed connection).
- **Lilly's departure from China:** Oct 28, from **Beijing → Zurich** (changed per Lilly's direction — the trip now loops back to Beijing by Oct 25, so the final flight needs to be ex-Beijing, not ex-Shanghai as in the earlier draft; **exact flight time TBD, needs (re)booking/confirmation**).
- **Emmanuel:** EU/French citizen — 30 days visa-free in China, no visa process needed. Window: Sept 30 → ~Oct 30.
- **After the joint trip:** Emmanuel continues on to Japan for ~1 month.
- **Lilly:** returns to Zurich, starts work **Nov 4**.
- Total time in China: 28 nights (Sept 30–Oct 28). Beijing leg uses 8 of those nights (fixed, Sept 30–Oct 8, matching Emmanuel's own draft calendar); 20 nights left for the southbound rail loop.
- **Oct 1–7 = Golden Week** (China's peak domestic travel holiday) — overlaps almost the entire Beijing stay (Oct 8 itself is just the travel day out). Expect major crowds/inflated prices; book timed-entry tickets (Forbidden City, Great Wall) well in advance.

## Route Preference
Rail-only (HSR G/D/C class) strongly preferred, **always the shortest available train on a given route** (not just any compliant option), no arrivals after 22:30, except explicitly-flagged car/bus transfers where no rail exists. Departure-time floor (revised per Lilly's direction): **Beijing departures after 10am**, and **from Beijing South or Beijing Fengtai, not Beijing West** (her parents live in Daxing); **every other city, departures ideally after 8:30am** (loosened from the earlier blanket 9am floor). Balance of culture, nature, cities, cuisine, and tech (Emmanuel: outdoorsy + culture/history + interested in China's tech scene).

## The Itinerary (12306-verified where noted; some legs flagged as approximate/bus)

Beijing → Xi'an (1-night stopover) → Chongqing → **Wulong Karst** (1-night stopover) → Chongqing → Kunming (+ **Shilin** and **Dali** day trips) → Jianshui → **Yuanyang** (bus, flagged) → Kunming (1-night return stopover, flagged) → Guilin → **Longji** (day trip, bus, flagged) → Yangshuo → Guilin (1-night return stopover) → Shanghai (+ **Suzhou** day trip) → **Beijing** (final — depart Oct 28 to Zurich).

Rebuilt from the two-itinerary draft per Lilly's direction: single itinerary only (no numbering, no tabs). Dali is now a same-day round trip from Kunming rather than its own multi-night stop — Kunming↔Dali is an easy ~2h each way by frequent D-class HSR, so it slots in alongside the existing Shilin day trip without adding a dead-end spur to the route. The Kunming-area cluster reads as: Kunming (Shilin day trip, Dali day trip) → Jianshui → Yuanyang → back through Kunming (1-night stopover) → on to Guilin. Since Yuanyang has no rail at all, the route always returns to Kunming — the nearest HSR hub — before continuing by proper HSR, rather than chaining bus and rail straight through to Guilin in one very long day. Shilin, Dali, Longji, and Wulong Karst are all added as day trips (not relocations), so none of them costs extra nights beyond what's needed to fit them.

This revision (on top of the single-route rebuild) applies four changes confirmed by Lilly via live 12306 research: (1) Beijing departure shifts one day later, to Oct 8, to match Emmanuel's own draft calendar; (2) Xi'an is cut to a genuine 1-night stopover — Terracotta Army is deprioritized given Golden Week crowds, in favor of a quick City Wall/old town/food evening; (3) Chongqing is extended by a night to fit a full-day Wulong Karst trip (Three Natural Bridges, Furong Cave — part of the UNESCO South China Karst site), chosen over Zhangjiajie for being less touristy and scenically distinct, and made easy by the new Chongqing East → Wulong South HSR link (~38 min); (4) the Kunming overnight between Yuanyang and Dali is dropped — see the Dali rework below, which supersedes this point. Net effect of these three still-standing changes: +1 night Beijing, −1 night Xi'an, +1 night Chongqing — these cancel out exactly, so nothing shifts through the end of the Chongqing stay.

**Dali → Kunming day trip (earlier revision):** Dali is reworked from a 2-night dead-end stop (reached via a Kunming pass-through) into a single-day round trip from Kunming itself, folded into the same Kunming stay as the Shilin day trip. Kunming grows from 2 to 3 nights to host both day trips; the old Yuanyang→Dali leg and the old Dali→Kunming→Guilin leg collapse into a single leg, Yuanyang→Guilin via Kunming (same-day transit, no overnight, continuing straight on to Guilin instead of stopping at Dali). Net effect versus the prior build: Kunming +1 night, Dali −2 nights (dropped as a stand-alone stop) — a net one day freed up, added to Shanghai (3→4 nights) at the time. **This freed day has since been reassigned — see the next revision.**

**Latest revision (Kunming overnight restored on the return leg):** The same-day Yuanyang→Guilin sprint (bus to Jianshui, HSR to Kunming, big layover, HSR on to Guilin, ~16h door-to-door with a ~05:30 bus departure) was the one leg that broke the "no pre-8am" rule and was never realistic to book as drawn. Fixed per Lilly's direction: **the route always comes back to Kunming — the nearest HSR hub — before continuing on**, since Yuanyang has no rail at all. Yuanyang→Kunming (bus + C-class HSR) is now its own travel day, followed by a Kunming overnight, then Kunming→Guilin (G4108, 17:10–21:49 — corrected from an initially mislabeled G910, see the 12306-verification revision below) the next afternoon — both legs comfortably inside the 8am–22:30 window. This costs one more night than the same-day version, so the day previously freed up by the Dali rework and given to Shanghai is redirected here instead: **Shanghai reverts to 3 nights (4→3)**, keeping the total at 28 nights, Sept 30 → Oct 28, with the fixed Oct 28, 20:30 Shanghai departure unchanged. Every date from Longji onward shifts one day later versus the prior build.

**Latest revision (Guilin restored as the return hub before Shanghai; 9am floor):** Same fix, second application. The Yangshuo→Shanghai leg was modeled as a same-day Yangshuo→Guilin→Shanghai pass-through (D8262 08:57 + G264 12:05) — but Guilin is always the start/end point for this cluster (Longji and Yangshuo both radiate from and return to it), so per Lilly's direction it now gets the same treatment as Kunming: an overnight stop, not a rushed connection. Yangshuo→Guilin and Guilin→Shanghai are now separate travel days. This costs one more night, funded by trimming Yangshuo from 3 to 2 nights (rather than compressing Shanghai a second time, having already gone 4→3 in the prior revision) — total stays at 28 nights, dates from Oct 23 onward unaffected beyond the split itself. Separately, the **"no pre-8am" rule tightens to "no pre-9am"** per Lilly's direction, which retroactively flags two existing legs as non-compliant pending 12306 re-verification: Yangshuo→Guilin (D8262, 08:57) and Kunming→Jianshui (C552, 08:23, previously justified as the only viable train before an 18:36 gap — may still be the one unavoidable exception).

**Latest revision (Beijing loop-back replaces the Shanghai finish):** Per Lilly's direction, the trip no longer ends in Shanghai — she needs to be back in Beijing from Shanghai by Oct 26 at the latest, with Oct 27–28 spent in Beijing before departure. This reverses the earlier "no loop back to Beijing" design. Concretely: Suzhou converts from a 1-night overnight loop into a same-day round trip from Shanghai (the HSR hop is only ~25–30min each way, so this loses nothing but the night); Shanghai trims from 3 to 2 nights (Oct 24–26); and a new Shanghai→Beijing HSR leg runs Oct 26. Beijing gets a second, final stay (Oct 26–28, 2 nights) before the Oct 28 departure. Net effect: −1 night Suzhou (folded into a day trip), −1 night Shanghai, +2 nights Beijing (final) — nets to zero, so the total stays at 28 nights, Sept 30 → Oct 28. **The Oct 28 departure flight needs to change from ex-Shanghai to ex-Beijing** — flagged as an open question since Lilly may need to rebook if the Shanghai flight was already ticketed. The Shanghai→Beijing leg itself is not yet 12306-verified (new leg, pending the next verification pass), and per Lilly's added station preference, Beijing-bound arrivals should favor Beijing South or Beijing Fengtai over Beijing West, since her parents live in Daxing.

**Two legs are genuinely not rail** and are flagged accordingly: Jianshui↔Yuanyang (no train serves Yuanyang at all — bus/car only, ~3h each way) and Guilin↔Longji (shuttle bus, ~2h45m–3h each way, no rail line). Everything else is HSR/intercity rail (G/D/C class), including the new Chongqing↔Wulong hop.

**Total rail time: ~50h across 17 rail/bus legs** (15 rail legs + 2 flagged bus-inclusive legs, plus the Shilin, Dali, and Suzhou day-trip round trips — Wulong is no longer a day trip, see below). Stay lengths: Xi'an 1 night, Chongqing 1+1 (split), Wulong Karst 1, Kunming 3 (+ 1 return stopover on the way to Guilin), Jianshui 1, Yuanyang 1, Guilin 2 (+ 1 return stopover on the way to Shanghai), Yangshuo 2, Shanghai 2 — 17 nights on the loop + 8 in Beijing (start) + 3 in Beijing (final) = 28 nights total, Sept 30 → Oct 28, matching the fixed trip length exactly. Dali no longer holds any nights of its own — folding it into a Kunming day trip freed up one day that funded the Kunming return stopover; the Guilin return stopover is funded by trimming Yangshuo; and the extra Beijing-final night (2→3) is funded by turning Wulong Karst from a day trip into an overnight, which itself was funded by trimming Chongqing's block from 4 nights to 2 (split around the Wulong stay).

**Latest revision (Wulong Karst becomes an overnight, not a day trip):** Lilly felt the Chongqing block (4 nights) was too long, and asked whether Wulong Karst could be an overnight instead of a rushed day trip. Recommended and implemented: Chongqing splits into two 1-night stays (Oct 9–10, Oct 11–12) bracketing a Wulong Karst overnight (Oct 10–11), rather than one 4-night Chongqing block with Wulong squeezed in as a same-day round trip that only really allowed time for one of Three Natural Bridges or Fairy Mountain, not both. Hotels are confirmed well-available in Xiannüshan (Fairy Mountain) town, the right base — not the HSR station itself, which is ~30–50min away by shuttle. Net effect: Chongqing+Wulong drops from 4 nights to 3 (1+1+1), freeing one night, which is redirected to Beijing's final stay (2→3 nights) rather than compressing anything else. Every date from Oct 12 (Chongqing→Kunming) onward shifts one day earlier versus the prior build, except Beijing's final stay, which now runs Oct 25–28 instead of Oct 26–28.

**Wulong Karst duration re-verified a third time** after Lilly independently found conflicting "~3 hour" travel-time claims online (matching a DeepSeek query she ran). Fully confirmed: the ~45min HSR figure (D3969, Chongqing East → Wulong South) is correct — it runs on the Yu-Xia HSR line that opened June 27, 2025, with ~20 direct HSR trains/day on this exact corridor (not a fluke single train). The "~3h" figures are real but describe the old pre-2025 conventional route (K-class trains to the old "Wulong" station) or road/bus transport — a traveler today should book to **Wulong South (武隆南)** specifically, not the old "Wulong" station. Door-to-door to the Xiannüshan/Three Natural Bridges scenic area, including the connecting shuttle bus, is closer to 1h15–1h30.

**Latest revision (full re-verification — shortest train always, tightened floor):** Per Lilly's direction, the rules tightened again and every leg was re-checked from scratch (parallel 12306 research, not trusting the prior pass): **always pick the shortest available train**, not just any compliant one; **Beijing departures after 10am, from Beijing South or Fengtai only** (not West — her parents live in Daxing); **every other city, after 8:30am** (loosened slightly from the blanket 9am floor). Net effect: several trains changed (Xi'an→Chongqing, all three day trips, Kunming→Guilin, Guilin→Yangshuo, Shanghai→Suzhou, Shanghai→Beijing), one earlier "correction" reversed (Kunming→Guilin really is G910, 09:13→13:41 — an earlier pass wrongly assumed that slot was unusable), and Lilly's specific skepticism about Wulong Karst's ~38min duration was checked in depth and confirmed correct (new 2025 HSR line, only ~85km). Three deliberate deviations from the literal "always shortest" rule, each flagged in its row: Wulong and Dali outbound both use a slightly-longer-but-much-earlier train to preserve daylight at the destination rather than the literal-shortest option; Jianshui→Kunming keeps the practical late-morning train over a 12-minutes-shorter one that would mean hours of dead time. **Both open conflicts are now resolved by Lilly:** Beijing→Xi'an couldn't satisfy both the 10am floor and the Fengtai/South station preference (no such train exists) — **she chose Beijing West** (G353, also faster: 4h13m vs. the old G1307's 5h49m). Kunming→Jianshui had only 2 Kunming-South-specific trains/day (fastest: C559, 18:36 evening); the full 8-train option list was re-researched (no G/D-class exists on this spur, only C and one slow K-class) and **she chose C552 (08:23, plain Kunming station)** for the early-afternoon Jianshui arrival, accepting the cross-city transfer away from Kunming South.
