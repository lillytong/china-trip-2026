# China Trip — Itinerary

## Travelers
- Lilly — Chinese-Canadian, born/raised in Beijing until age 11, later became Canadian citizen, now lives in Zurich.
- Emmanuel — French citizen, boyfriend. Max 30 days in China per current visa-free entry policy for France.

## Key Dates & Constraints
- **Arrival:** Sept 30, 10:30am — Beijing Daxing Airport (connecting flight; risk of missed connection).
- **Lilly's departure from China:** Oct 28, 20:30, **Shanghai → Zurich**. (Booked from Shanghai, not Beijing — the trip now ends in Shanghai, no loop back to Beijing at the end.)
- **Emmanuel:** EU/French citizen — 30 days visa-free in China, no visa process needed. Window: Sept 30 → ~Oct 30.
- **After the joint trip:** Emmanuel continues on to Japan for ~1 month.
- **Lilly:** returns to Zurich, starts work **Nov 4**.
- Total time in China: 28 nights (Sept 30–Oct 28). Beijing leg uses 8 of those nights (fixed, Sept 30–Oct 8, matching Emmanuel's own draft calendar); 20 nights left for the southbound rail loop.
- **Oct 1–7 = Golden Week** (China's peak domestic travel holiday) — overlaps almost the entire Beijing stay (Oct 8 itself is just the travel day out). Expect major crowds/inflated prices; book timed-entry tickets (Forbidden City, Great Wall) well in advance.

## Route Preference
Rail-only (HSR G/D/C class) strongly preferred — no departures before 8am, no arrivals after 22:30, except explicitly-flagged car/bus transfers where no rail exists. Balance of culture, nature, cities, cuisine, and tech (Emmanuel: outdoorsy + culture/history + interested in China's tech scene).

## The Itinerary (12306-verified where noted; some legs flagged as approximate/bus)

Beijing → Xi'an (1-night stopover) → Chongqing (+ **Wulong Karst** day trip) → Kunming (+ **Shilin** and **Dali** day trips) → Jianshui → **Yuanyang** (bus, flagged) → Guilin (via Kunming, same-day transit, no overnight, flagged) → **Longji** (day trip, bus, flagged) → Yangshuo → Guilin (transit) → Shanghai → **Suzhou** (1 night) → Shanghai (final — depart Oct 28, 20:30 to Zurich).

Rebuilt from the two-itinerary draft per Lilly's direction: single itinerary only (no numbering, no tabs). Dali is now a same-day round trip from Kunming rather than its own multi-night stop — Kunming↔Dali is an easy ~2h each way by frequent D-class HSR, so it slots in alongside the existing Shilin day trip without adding a dead-end spur to the route. The Kunming-area cluster reads as: Kunming (Shilin day trip, Dali day trip) → Jianshui → Yuanyang → transit through Kunming (same-day, no overnight) → straight on to Guilin. Shilin, Dali, Longji, and Wulong Karst are all added as day trips (not relocations), so none of them costs extra nights beyond what's needed to fit them.

This revision (on top of the single-route rebuild) applies four changes confirmed by Lilly via live 12306 research: (1) Beijing departure shifts one day later, to Oct 8, to match Emmanuel's own draft calendar; (2) Xi'an is cut to a genuine 1-night stopover — Terracotta Army is deprioritized given Golden Week crowds, in favor of a quick City Wall/old town/food evening; (3) Chongqing is extended by a night to fit a full-day Wulong Karst trip (Three Natural Bridges, Furong Cave — part of the UNESCO South China Karst site), chosen over Zhangjiajie for being less touristy and scenically distinct, and made easy by the new Chongqing East → Wulong South HSR link (~38 min); (4) the Kunming overnight between Yuanyang and Dali is dropped — see the Dali rework below, which supersedes this point. Net effect of these three still-standing changes: +1 night Beijing, −1 night Xi'an, +1 night Chongqing — these cancel out exactly, so nothing shifts through the end of the Chongqing stay.

**Latest revision (Dali → Kunming day trip):** Dali is reworked from a 2-night dead-end stop (reached via a Kunming pass-through) into a single-day round trip from Kunming itself, folded into the same Kunming stay as the Shilin day trip. Kunming grows from 2 to 3 nights to host both day trips; the old Yuanyang→Dali leg and the old Dali→Kunming→Guilin leg collapse into a single leg, Yuanyang→Guilin via Kunming (same-day transit, no overnight, continuing straight on to Guilin instead of stopping at Dali). Net effect versus the prior build: Kunming +1 night, Dali −2 nights (dropped as a stand-alone stop) — a net one day freed up. That freed day is added to Shanghai (3→4 nights) rather than left as a schedule gap, keeping the total at 28 nights, Sept 30 → Oct 28, with the fixed Oct 28, 20:30 Shanghai departure unchanged.

**Two legs are genuinely not rail** and are flagged accordingly: Jianshui↔Yuanyang (no train serves Yuanyang at all — bus/car only, ~3h each way) and Guilin↔Longji (shuttle bus, ~2h45m–3h each way, no rail line). Everything else is HSR/intercity rail (G/D/C class), including the new Chongqing↔Wulong hop.

**Total rail time: ~34h across 12 rail/bus legs** (10 rail legs + 2 flagged bus-inclusive legs, plus the Wulong and Dali day-trip round trips). Stay lengths: Xi'an 1 night, Chongqing 4, Kunming 3 (+ a same-day transit pass on the way to Guilin), Jianshui 1, Yuanyang 1, Guilin 2 (+ a same-day transit pass on the way to Shanghai), Yangshuo 3, Shanghai 4, Suzhou 1 — 20 nights on the loop + 8 in Beijing = 28 nights total, Sept 30 → Oct 28, matching the fixed trip length exactly. Dali no longer holds any nights of its own — folding it into a Kunming day trip freed up one day versus the prior 2-night dead-end stop, and that freed day was added to Shanghai (3→4 nights) rather than left as a gap.

**On the "no pre-8am, no post-22:30" rule:** every rail leg above holds to it, **with one exception**: the Yuanyang→Guilin same-day run on Oct 18 needs an early-morning bus out of Yuanyang (~05:30–06:00) to make the 09:02 Jianshui→Kunming train — the same trade-off as before, just now carried through all the way to Guilin instead of stopping at Dali. It's a very long day (roughly 16 hours door-to-door, most of it a layover in Kunming), but every actual departure is at/after 8am and the last arrival (G910 into Guilin West) lands at 21:38, under the 22:30 cutoff. One other combined travel day runs long by necessity (Yangshuo→Guilin→Shanghai on Oct 23) but still respects the same rule — a full travel day with little else planned, flagged in the table above.
