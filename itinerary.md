## Travelers
- Lilly — Chinese-Canadian, born/raised in Beijing until age 11, later became Canadian citizen, now lives in Zurich.
- Emmanuel — French citizen, boyfriend. Max 30 days in China per current visa-free entry policy for France.

## Key Dates & Constraints
- **Arrival:** Sept 30, 10:30am — Beijing Daxing Airport (connecting flight; risk of missed connection).
- **Lilly's departure from China:** Oct 28, from **Beijing → Zurich** (changed per Lilly's direction — the trip now loops back to Beijing by Oct 26, so the final flight needs to be ex-Beijing, not ex-Shanghai as in the earlier draft; **exact flight time TBD, needs (re)booking/confirmation**).

- **After the joint trip:** Emmanuel continues on to Japan for ~1 month.
- **Lilly:** returns to Zurich, starts work **Nov 4**.
- Total time in China: 28 nights (Sept 30–Oct 28). Beijing leg uses 8 of those nights (fixed, Sept 30–Oct 8, matching Emmanuel's own draft calendar); 20 nights left for the southbound rail loop.
- **Oct 1–7 = Golden Week** (China's peak domestic travel holiday) — overlaps almost the entire Beijing stay (Oct 8 itself is just the travel day out). Expect major crowds/inflated prices; book timed-entry tickets (Forbidden City, Great Wall) well in advance.

## Route Preference
Rail-only (HSR G/D/C class) strongly preferred, **always the shortest available train on a given route** (not just any compliant option), no arrivals after 22:30, except explicitly-flagged car/bus transfers where no rail exists. Departure-time floor (revised per Lilly's direction): **Beijing departures after 10am**, and **from Beijing South or Beijing Fengtai, not Beijing West** (her parents live in Daxing); **every other city, departures ideally after 8:30am** (loosened from the earlier blanket 9am floor). Balance of culture, nature, cities, cuisine, and tech (Emmanuel: outdoorsy + culture/history + interested in China's tech scene). **Final verification always goes through 12306.cn itself**, not aggregators (Ctrip, Trip.com, Gaotie.com.cn, etc.) — aggregators are fine for a first pass while a date is still outside 12306's ~15-day booking window, but every leg gets a direct 12306 check once it's inside that window, before booking.
